**Long description (AlternativeTo, BetaList):**
Munymo is a free daily game for learning how the stock market works by making one decision a day.

Every US trading day there is a single matchup between two real, listed companies in the same sector, tied to a real news story from the last couple of days. Everyone plays the same matchup.

How a game works:
1. Gut pick. Choose which company you think will perform better, on instinct alone.
2. Research. Read a plain-English brief comparing the two companies, with key metrics and a price chart. The brief is written fresh each day by an AI research agent from live sources, and the source article is credited and linked.
3. Final pick. Lock in your scored prediction and answer one question that checks you read the research.
4. Result. After the close, the winner is settled from published open and close prices. You see how your gut pick compared with your researched pick, and how other players chose.

There is no real money and nothing to buy or sell. Scores, streaks and a leaderboard keep it competitive. A free Learning Hub of 32 short lessons covers the basics, from what a share is to reading valuation metrics, and every past game stays available in a free archive.

Munymo is in open beta and free to play.
